The AIChE Southern Asia Parliamentary Debate Competition 2025 brings together student chapters to debate engineering and sustainability topics.

Date: 2 December 2025

Time: 09:00 (GMT+6)

Venue: Online (Zoom)

Register Now

Event Information

Arranged by: Southern Asia Regional Subcommittee, AIChE ESC

Event website: aichesouthernasia.github.io

Objective

To foster critical thinking, persuasive communication, and teamwork among AIChE student members through structured debates on engineering and sustainability topics.

1. Eligibility & Participation

  • Eligibility: Open to all active AIChE student chapters. All participants must hold valid AIChE student membership at the time of the event.
  • Team Registration: Each participating chapter may register a maximum of three teams.
  • Team Composition: Each team consists of two speakers from the same chapter:
    • Both speakers are required to participate.
  • Substitution: Speaker substitution after registration is not permitted except under exceptional circumstances approved by the organizing committee.
  • Grouping: Participating chapters will be randomly assigned to either Proposition or Opposition. Party assignments are final. In case of an uneven number of teams, scores will be normalized to ensure fairness.
  • Communication: All selected participants will be added to a dedicated WhatsApp group for updates, coordination, announcements, speaking order, and Zoom details.

2. Debate Motion

  • The official motion is:
    “From Landfills to Power Plants: South Asia Must Adopt Waste-to-Energy Systems to Solve Its Mounting Urban Waste Problem and Energy Deficit.”
  • Teams must argue strictly in favor of or against the motion as assigned. Reframing the motion is not allowed.
  • Arguments must directly address the motion within the South Asian context, focusing on engineering and sustainability issues.

3. Debate Format

  • Style: Basic Proposition–Opposition parliamentary format.
  • Speaking Time: Each speaker gets 3 minutes 30 seconds (warning after 3 minutes).
    • Speakers must present in the assigned order (communicated via WhatsApp).
    • Up to 15 seconds over: warning
    • Beyond 15 seconds: point deduction at judges’ discretion
  • No interruptions, cross-talk, or screen-sharing allowed during speeches unless instructed.
  • Use of notes is permitted; reading verbatim may negatively affect delivery scores.

4. Online Conduct (Zoom)

  • Join using real name and registered chapter name.
  • Video must remain on throughout the event.
  • Microphone muted except when speaking.
  • Ensure stable internet; technical issues handled at organizers’ discretion.
  • Join at least 10 minutes early. Late arrival or disruptions may incur penalties or disqualification.

5. Judging Criteria (Total 100 points)

CriteriaDescriptionPoints
Content & LogicStrength, structure, depth, and evidence-based arguments30
Team CoordinationClear division, logical continuity, and consistent stance25
Delivery & ConfidenceClarity, tone, confidence, and stage presence25
Relevance & ImpactConnection to engineering/sustainability and South Asian context20

6. Conduct & Penalties

  • Personal attacks, offensive language, disrespect, or knowingly false claims may lead to penalties or disqualification.
  • Judges’ decisions are final; no appeals.

7. Scoring & Recognition

  • Evaluation: Judged by members of the AIChE Executive Committee.
  • Awards:
    • Winning Team (highest total points)
    • Best Speaker (individual performance)
  • Recognition: Winners featured on AIChE Executive Committee social media platforms.
  • Participation Certificates: All participants will receive digital certificates from AIChE ESC.
  • Impact: Participating chapters earn AIChE Activity Points toward annual evaluation and active chapter status.
